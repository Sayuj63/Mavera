About Mavera Impex

A steady bridge between quality supply and global care.

Mavera Impex is a globally operating pharmaceutical import and export company, bridging the gap between GMP-certified manufacturers and pharmaceutical buyers across Emerging, Regulated & ROW markets. We specialize in end-to-end trade facilitation from sourcing and quality verification to documentation, cold-chain logistics, and last-mile delivery.

Who we are

Mavera Impex is a pharmaceutical indenting and sourcing partner based in Mumbai, India, connecting accredited Indian manufacturers with pharmaceutical buyers across Emerging, Regulated and ROW markets.

We help pharmaceutical companies, finished-formulation manufacturers and healthcare businesses identify reliable, GMP-compliant supply partners and source quality products directly from India & China.

Our product portfolio includes APIs, Excipients, Ready-to-Fill Pellets & MUPS, Finished Formulations, and Medical & Healthcare Supplies.

With a strong network of trusted manufacturers and an understanding of international pharmaceutical requirements, we support our partners with quality-focused sourcing, regulatory documentation, competitive commercial solutions and dependable supply coordination.
