About Mavera Impex

A steady bridge between quality supply and global care.

We are a pharmaceutical import and export company built around dependable relationships, disciplined execution and a clear understanding of how healthcare products move across borders.

Our background

Trade knowledge, made accountable.

Mavera Impex was created to give international distributors, hospitals, pharmacies and manufacturing partners a more direct way to access quality-assured pharmaceutical and healthcare products.

We bring together supplier identification, commercial coordination and export support, helping each requirement move from an early brief to a practical, documented supply plan. Our India and China offices give us a grounded view of regional manufacturing and international market needs.

Our commitment

We respect the standards that healthcare demands. Every partnership is approached with care for product integrity, clear communication and the long-term reputation of the teams we serve.

Who we work with

  • International distributors
  • Hospital and clinic networks
  • Retail pharmacy groups
  • Healthcare manufacturers

Where we add value

Product discovery, supplier alignment, export documentation, shipment coordination and ongoing commercial support.

Healthcare-first mindset

We move products that support real clinical outcomes.

Behind every shipment there is a pharmacist, a clinician or a patient who is counting on it. That awareness shapes how we choose suppliers, structure documentation and follow through on commitments.
